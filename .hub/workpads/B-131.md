## What changed and where

- **`tests/testthat/test-fraser-recruits-internals-guard.R`**, new. Three tests
  over two lists:
  1. each of the eight names resolves the way `metasalmon:::name` resolves it
     (`get0(name, envir = asNamespace("metasalmon"), inherits = FALSE)`, which
     is what `:::` does) to a function;
  2. each keeps its formal argument names, in order, as they stand on `main`
     at `fb1a776`;
  3. `.ms_eml_validate_mapping()` keeps `require_final = TRUE`, the only
     default among the eight.

  The header states the obligation; names the S-13 card as the list's home and
  says the two change together; says what is pinned and what is not, and why;
  states the guard's real scope, measured below; and gives each name its own
  retirement condition.
- Nothing else. No export, no signature change, no `R/` edit, and no `NEWS.md`
  entry, because a test is not a user-visible change.

**Why the one default is pinned without seeing the call.** The recipe's call
sites are not in this repository, and the S-13 card records what each call is
for rather than its arguments, so whether the recipe omits `require_final`
could not be observed. It is pinned anyway because it sets how strict the
validation is: flipping it would silently loosen a call that omits it, where a
rename at least fails at the call. Return values and bodies are not pinned.
They change by design (`.ms_sdp_profile_version()` moves with the vendored
spec), and pinning them would freeze internals rather than guard a name.
