## What changed and where

- **`tests/testthat/test-vignette-purl-guard.R`** (new). Two tests.
  - **The real vignettes.** For every vignette `tools::pkgVignettes()` finds,
    the guard reads each `knitr::opts_chunk$set()` call in its R chunks without
    running it. A vignette **relies on a global option** when one of those calls
    sets `eval` or `purl` to anything but the literal `TRUE`, or uses
    `opts_chunk` in a way the guard cannot read. That second part is
    conservative on purpose. Each vignette is then tangled the way
    `R CMD check`'s fresh process tangles it: `tools::vignetteEngine()`'s own
    tangle function runs on a scratch copy of `vignettes/`, with knitr's chunk
    options reset to their defaults. The guard fails when a vignette that relies
    on a global option still tangles to a live R expression. The message names
    each live chunk by its source line and first statement. A vignette in any
    format other than R Markdown fails instead of passing unread. The test also
    fails when the scan finds no vignettes at all.
  - **The guard's own semantics**, on ten fixture vignettes written to a temp
    directory. Four cases must be flagged: a bare chunk under a global
    `eval = FALSE` (this item's shape), the setup chunk purled instead, a bare
    chunk under a global `purl = FALSE`, and the options passed as one `list()`.
    Two more are flagged because the guard reads them conservatively: a
    computed `eval`, and `do.call(knitr::opts_chunk$set, ...)`. Four cases must
    pass: `purl = FALSE` in the header (#32's fix), `eval = FALSE` in the header,
    `#| purl: false` inside the chunk, and an executing vignette that sets no
    global `eval` or `purl`. This test is the RED demonstration that survives
    B-133. Once both vignettes are fixed, nothing real is left to show the guard
    can fail, and these cases still show it.
  - **`known_offenders`** names the two vignettes against B-133. It is a record
    and not an exemption. A listed vignette that stops offending fails the test
    until its entry is deleted, and an offender that is not listed fails.
- **`NEWS.md`**: a new `### Internal` heading under the development version,
  with one entry.
- **`knowledge/backlog.md`**:
  - #32's entry gains a line saying the rule is now guarded, and by what.
