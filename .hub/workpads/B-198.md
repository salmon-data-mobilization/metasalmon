## Guards added, and what retires each

- **The manifest test.** It retires when `inst/extdata` stops vendoring a
  copy of an upstream spec release, because the hashes are what make "a copy"
  checkable. Its pin clause alone retires sooner, if the loader stops fetching
  a remote bundle. This is stated in the test. The manifest itself is not a
  guard. It advances with the pin.
- **The network test's skips** (`skip_on_cran`, `skip_if_offline`, a transport
  error, 429 or 5xx). They retire when the loader stops fetching a remote
  bundle, or stops falling back to the vendored one. This is stated in the
  test.
- **The four-digit-year exclusion** in the temporal test. It retires when
  B-161 is ruled and B-206 emits the ruled pre-1000 spelling. This is stated
  in the test file's header.
- **Retired in round 2: the commit pin.** Its condition, "smn-data-pkg tags a
  release at or after `f86d9b4` and the pin moves to that tag", is met.
