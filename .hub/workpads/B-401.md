# B-401 — Correct the NuSEDS AREA description

## Change

The `AREA` row in each bundled NuSEDS column dictionary now says DFO sub-district. The current S12, S9, Q5 and roadmap passages no longer treat the extract's lettered `29F`, `29G`, `29J` and `29K` values as PFMA Subareas. Q8's routing for actual PFMA Subareas remains a separate ruling. The need for a sub-district term or a `gcdfo` carve-out remains open for Brett and the commons gap register. ## Source and defect evidence

- DFO's NuSEDS English data dictionary, `Data_Dictionary_NuSEDS_EN.csv`, SHA-256 `195a164956d6c748f70856111d48ba9ca6c7d6ae2080736411b89c975bda08f0`, has an `AREA` record defining the field as a subdistrict. The published DFO map, SHA-256 `bee96f397c6365a4507c9209d0bc0709dfe141c9a691cd55beb1a788e9073f41`, is headed "Pacific Region Sub-Districts" and names 29F Lillooet, 29G Williams Lake, 29J Clearwater and 29K Salmon Arm. The official Pacific Fishery Management Area Regulations enumerate Area 29 Subareas as 29-1 onward. The source synthesis is in `salmon-knowledge-commons/concepts/nuseds-area-is-a-subdistrict.md`, marked draft and not human-verified.
- Before editing, the two bundled `AREA` rows labelled the field "PFMA area" and described it as a Pacific Fishery Management Area code. The S12, Q5, roadmap and S9 passages assigned those lettered values to PFMA Subareas. A post-edit CSV comparison with HEAD confirms only `column_label` and `column_description` changed in the `AREA` row of each file; 17 and 14 rows remain respectively.
